# Global instructions

## Language

- Speak to me in French: all explanations, summaries, and chat responses.
- Everything written into the codebase must be in English: code, comments,
  docstrings, commit messages, log output, identifiers, and documentation.

## Grain

- Aim for one graspable idea per unit of space — per paragraph, per function,
  per comment. Density should match span: don't compress several ideas into a
  dense line, don't stretch one idea thin to fill a page.
- Too much in too little space is unreadable; too little across too much space
  is noise with nothing to hold onto. Neither is simple.
- Work in small, constructive steps. Prefer the next clear step over clever
  compression or exhaustive coverage — in prose, in code, and in how you
  proceed.

## Git / commits

- Do not commit or push unless I explicitly ask. After finishing work, stop and
  let me review the diff — don't offer to commit by default.
- When I do ask to commit, do it in one step without re-confirming.
