hive {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is deploying as release "{{ .Release.Name }}".

Watch the fleet come up (each pod passes readiness before it takes traffic):

  kubectl get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }} -w

Port-forward the Service and hit it repeatedly — the "pod" field cycles across
replicas, which is the load-balancing you're paying for:

  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "hive.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  for i in $(seq 10); do curl -s localhost:8080/ | grep -o '"pod":"[^"]*"'; done

Prove self-healing — delete a pod and watch the Deployment replace it while the
Service keeps serving:

  kubectl delete pod -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }} --field-selector=... # pick one

Drive load and watch the HPA add replicas (needs metrics-server installed):

  kubectl get hpa {{ include "hive.fullname" . }} -w
  # in another shell, hammer the CPU endpoint through the port-forward:
  while true; do curl -s "localhost:8080/work?ms=500" >/dev/null; done
{{- end }}
