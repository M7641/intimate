# Russian Grammar A1–C2 — split edition

The complete guide has been divided into eight smaller volumes. Read them in order for a course-like progression, or open the final reference volume for quick lookup.

## Reading order

1. **Foundations and Grammar Map** — Sound, spelling, sentence structure, cases, gender and animacy. Word: `Word/01_Foundations_and_Grammar_Map.docx`; Markdown: `Markdown/01_Foundations_and_Grammar_Map.md`.
2. **A1 Core Sentences** — The grammar needed for first conversations and descriptions. Word: `Word/02_A1_Core_Sentences.docx`; Markdown: `Markdown/02_A1_Core_Sentences.md`.
3. **A2 Everyday Grammar** — Completing the core case, aspect and motion systems. Word: `Word/03_A2_Everyday_Grammar.docx`; Markdown: `Markdown/03_A2_Everyday_Grammar.md`.
4. **B1 Narration and Explanation** — Building coherent stories, reasons, conditions and relative clauses. Word: `Word/04_B1_Narration_and_Explanation.docx`; Markdown: `Markdown/04_B1_Narration_and_Explanation.md`.
5. **B2 Compression and Nuance** — Participles, gerunds, government, particles and formal structures. Word: `Word/05_B2_Compression_and_Nuance.docx`; Markdown: `Markdown/05_B2_Compression_and_Nuance.md`.
6. **C1 Precision and Register** — Fine meaning differences, formal syntax and discourse control. Word: `Word/06_C1_Precision_and_Register.docx`; Markdown: `Markdown/06_C1_Precision_and_Register.md`.
7. **C2 Stylistic Control** — Idiomatic grammar, register transformation and editorial judgement. Word: `Word/07_C2_Stylistic_Control.docx`; Markdown: `Markdown/07_C2_Stylistic_Control.md`.
8. **Reference Tables and Study Plan** — Declensions, conjugations, aspect, motion, numerals and level checklists. Word: `Word/08_Reference_Tables_and_Study_Plan.docx`; Markdown: `Markdown/08_Reference_Tables_and_Study_Plan.md`.

The original single-file guide is intentionally left unchanged outside this folder.
