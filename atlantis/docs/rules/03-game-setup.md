# Game Setup (4 or 6 players)

**[p. 5]**

*Figure: the board set up for play, spread across pages 5-6. Numbers 1 and 3
to 11 mark where each setup step happens; step 2 has no place on the board. Labels: "Blue" Throne, "Red" Throne.*

## 1. Place the Board

Take the board and place it as shown.

## 2. Split into Two Equal Teams

Players must split into two equal teams. One team is "Team red" and the other
team is "Team blue".

## 3. Place the Tie Breaker Coin

Take the Tie Breaker coin, flip it in the air, and then place it, result side
up, near the corner of the board, as shown.

*Figure: Tie Breaker coin.*

## 4. Place the Wave Counters

Place a number of Wave counters next to the edge of the board based on your
desired game length:

**Quick game:** 3 Wave counters.

**Long game:** 5 Wave counters.

*Figure: Wave counter.*

> **Note:** If any players at your table are new, use the Quick Game Setup. I
> know, I know, you are all experienced board gamers and want to skip the baby
> steps, but you do want your friends to have a fun time, right?

## 5. Place the Life Counters

Take a number of blue and red Life counters based on your chosen game length
and the total player count, as shown in the table below.

| Player Count | Quick game | Long game |
| ------------ | ---------- | --------- |
| 4 Players    | 4          | 6         |
| 6 Players    | 5          | 8         |

Place the Life counters on the corresponding Throne with their "unspent" side
up. Return the rest to the box.

*Figure: The Blue and the Red Life counters (Unspent/Spent).*

## 6. Place the Coins

Take the coins and place them within reach of all players.

*Figure: Coins.*

## 7. Distribute Hero Dashboards

In a game with 4 players, take the hero dashboards for "Wasp the Warmaiden" and
"Xargatha the Changed" and distribute them randomly to the players on the Red
team. Do the same with the dashboards for "Arien the Tidemaster" and "Brogan
the Destroyer" for the Blue team. Place the dashboards in front of the players.

In a game with 6 players, use the dashboards for "Tigerclaw the Cutpurse" and
"Sabina the Gunslinger" for the third players on the Red and Blue teams,
respectively.

> **Note:** In future games, you can use any of the methods described on page
> 22 for selecting heroes, but for your first game, use the recommended line-up
> above. Trust me on this.

## 8. Place the Level Indicator Cards

Each player takes a Level indicator card and tucks it under the left edge of
their hero dashboard to indicate their starting hero level (Level 1).

**[p. 6]**

## 9. Place Hero Miniatures

Attach the colored bases of the corresponding color to the hero miniatures.

Place the hero miniatures on the marked spaces in their corresponding Throne
Zone (highlighted in Blue and Red). These spaces are called hero spawn points.

*Figure: Hero Spawn points.*

> **Note:** Your hero's miniature is depicted on the back of your hero
> dashboard, along with tactical tips for playing that hero and their
> backstory.

## 10. Place Minion Miniatures

There are three types of minions in *Guards of Atlantis II*: Ranged, Melee, and
Heavy (more on that later).

Place the minions on the board in the starting Battle Zone (highlighted in
white) on spaces marked with a minion icon that matches the team color and the
type of that minion. These icons are the minion spawn points.

The image to the right shows which minion Spawn points correspond to which
minion miniature and how many of each miniature you need to place during the
setup.

*Figure: Blue minions with their spawn point icons: 1x Ranged, 4x Melee, 1x
Heavy. Red minions with their spawn point icons: 1x Ranged, 4x Melee, 1x
Heavy.*

> **Note:** Keep unused ranged and heavy minions in the box.

## 11. Prepare Cards, Tokens and Markers

Each player takes their hero's deck of 18 Action cards. The symbol on the back
of the cards should match the symbol on their hero dashboard.

*Figure: a hero dashboard and a card back showing the same symbol.*

The Roman numeral in the top right corner of each Action card is its Tier. Find
the three Tier I cards and the two basic cards with no Tier in your deck.

*Figure: Starting Non-Basic cards. Three Tier I cards: a blue one ("Aspiring
Duelist", Initiative 9), a green one (Initiative 4, Skill - Ranged, "Place
yourself into a space in range without a spawn point and not adjacent to an
empty spawn point.") and a red Attack card, partly hidden.*

*Figure: Starting Basic cards. Two cards with no Tier: a silver one ("Spell
Break", Initiative 13) and a gold one (Initiative 11, Basic Attack 4, "Target a
unit adjacent to you. Before the attack: You may move another unit that is
adjacent to the target 1 space. ("Another unit" never includes you.)").*

You should end up with five Action cards; these are your starting cards. Take
them into your hand and return the remaining Tier II, Tier III, and Tier IV
cards to your deck. These will be used later when leveling up (See pages
15-16). Return the Handicap card ("H" Tier) to the box.

If your hero uses tokens or markers (See pages 18-19), it will be displayed on
the back of your hero dashboard. Take your Tokens and Markers and place them
near you.

> **Note:** You never draw cards from your deck, so there is no need to shuffle
> it.
