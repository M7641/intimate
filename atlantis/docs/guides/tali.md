## Introduction

[Image 6943765 on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/6943765)

Over the last couple weeks, you've heard about the crazy Brogan charges, the
high-flying antics of Misa, and the laundry list of crimes committed by
Tigerclaw. Those are all well and good, but maybe those flashy moves just aren't
your style.

Maybe you're the kind of player who is fine taking a back seat to those scene
stealers. Maybe you're more interested in assisting other's reach their full
potential than closing every kill. Maybe you're more content in knowing that you
did your part regardless of whether those efforts will bring any sort of
recognition.

If that's the case, let me introduce you to your new favorite fox: Tali the
Spiritcaller.

Tali is Guard's version of the "support" archetype from MOBAs. This means she
has a lot of ways to set up her team for kills (notice her stat card has a
decent amount in brawler), stabilizing the push, healing, and even some
additional support utility that toys with the normal structure of Guards.

Her biggest weaknesses are:

- Only one kill option. While she has incredibly good discard potential, only
  her Red can kill. Any character with this as a limitation requires extra
  coordination with their teammate to achieve a kill win.

- Her optimal round normally has her voluntarily discarding a card at some point
  in the game. This leaves her open to attacks.

- Her best tool - Silver - requires her teammates to know just as much about her
  kit as the player piloting her to reach her full potential.

### The Basics

**Gold: Turn Into Ice**

*Choose One:*

*-A hero in range with an unresolved card may choose to retrieve that card. If
they do not, name a color; that hero discards a card of that color, if able.*

*- Defeat a minion adjacent to you.*

This first choice has a lot going on, but really highlights what Tali has to
offer as a teammate. Against an enemy hero, this card can either be a full turn
stun at the cost of a heal (as they get to pick that card back up) or an
extremely powerful targeted discard.

While this is a very strong ability, know that when used on an enemy, more often
than not they will take the stun rather than face the nightmare of being forced
to discard their precious Red. However, when planned for, that can be just as
powerful an option.

While it can be niche, also notice that this card text also bolsters Tali's role
as a support. That first ability just specifies "hero", not "enemy", meaning
friendly heroes are also viable targets. In certain situations, this card can be
used to prevent a kill by having an ally retrieve their played card. This could
be huge in a scenario where they are being threatened by an attack with no
defense cards in hand.

**Silver: Commune With Spirits**

*The Nearest friendly hero names a color, you may then choose one -*

*- Discard a card of that color: If you do, perform any one action on that card*

*- Retrieve a discarded card of that color*

*Initiative 12*

Take in that first choice, because it's a doozy. In the simplest terms, this
card gives any card in Tali's kit a Gold initiative at the cost of her
"wounding" herself by discarding that card. The most obvious utility will be to
use this with your Red attacks, but there is much more being presented here.

The key to unlocking this Silver's full potential is in having a team that knows
Tali's cards as well as you do. While there is nothing stopping you from gently
telling the spirits "yo, say red or I'm never playing with you again," this card
reaches new heights when you can play it with full trust in your teammate. It is
one of the only instances in the game where you can bend the "no strategy talk"
rule after cards are revealed, as your chosen spirit can survey all flipped
cards before making their decision. In that way, this card is an embodiment of
her supportive nature. It's Tali asking: "What do you need from me this turn?"
You can't get much more support than that.

Also, make sure you and your team internalize that second option, because it can
be equally strong. Next to Whisper's Silver, this is one of the fastest self
heals in the game, and can really get you out of some hairy situations.

### The Branches

#### Green

Tali's Greens offer a lot of flexibility in whether she can support her team
through pushing or hero fights. In addition, as both use tokens, both branches
offer effective board control options as well.

It's also important to reiterate the flexibility your silver allows with these
cards. Played normally, these branches are great for setup. They allow you to
influence events in future turns in very effective ways. However, as you are
reading through both branches, consider the ways they could be used more
offensively when paired with the fast initiative of her silver.

**Primary Green: Pack Ice**

*Place up to 3 ice tokens in radius, adjacent to another ice token, if able.
Enemy heroes have -1 Initiative penalty for every ice token they are adjacent
to.*

To understand the effectiveness of this card, it helps to understand how
initiative -- and other values -- in Guards work. There is already [a great
article](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2887548/reference-sheets-and-why-you-dont-need-them)
about it that I recommend reading if you haven't, but the main thing to
understand for this branch is just how big even a 1/-1 initiative swing can be.
That 1 point can be the difference between an enemy escaping a heavy hitting Red
with their Blue, or exploding into sweet sweet coins for your team when they had
no choice but to take the hit.

**Alternate Green: Venerated Totem**

*Place a totem token in radius. If a friendly minion adjacent to a totem would
be defeated, you may remove the totem instead. Tokens are immune to enemy
actions.*

With this branch, we see that Tali is not content in just helping out her fellow
heroes. She cares about her minion pals too! This branch definitely has more
restrictions for placement than your primary, both in spots allowed and quantity
of tokens placed, but can still be hugely effective in a round.

Consider placing this early while enemy heroes are moving into position to farm
some minions, or save it for later and pair it with silver to deny a key minion
kill.

#### Blue

Where Tali's Green branches have a more clear cut utility between helping with
hero fights or push, Tali's Blues offer a different choice. Mainly, do you want
to patch your teammates up, or help them do a murder?

**Primary Blue: Blizzard**

*Name a color. The nearest enemy hero in radius discards a card of that color,
if able. End of next turn: That hero may retrieve a discarded card of that
color.*

This card right here is the payoff to the foreshadowing we saw in Tali's gold.
Is your team having trouble clearing that 8 defense on Brogan's Red? Did
Tigerclaw grab just the right Blue block card to keep him safe? No big deal.
We'll just take those out of the equation.

However, while this card is undeniably strong, it comes with an important
caveat: this discard is not permanent. At a certain point, enemies will be able
to get the named card back (when exactly this happens depends on tier.) Because
of this, you really need to coordinate with your teammates when playing this if
the ultimate goal is to land a kill. Also be aware that the target is the
nearest enemy hero. While you are still moving at a blue initiative, enemies can
potentially dive with golds or faster blues to force you to change the target
you were expecting to hit.

That's not all this card is good for though. Even without setting up a hero
kill, this is still getting a card out of an enemies hand for one to two full
turns depending on the tier. When used for this purpose it could mean blocking
an enemy from a minion kill, saving a teammate from a heavy attack, or severely
limiting an enemies movement options later in the round.

**Alternate Blue: Vengeful Spirit**

*If you have a card in the discard, you, or a friendly hero in radius, may
retrieve a discarded card.*

*Radius 4*

With Tali's Alt Blue, we see her supportive nature in it's most overt context.
Nothing too flashy going on here, but a high ini heal is a high ini heal. And
with some pretty crazy radius coverage as well! More to the point, it has a
restriction that you are in complete control over.

I find that heals tend to see more play in 3v3 when having that extra teammate
allows certain heroes to lean more into full support roles, and that's where I
think this card shines as well. However, it also has the benefit of being able
to undo the damage dealt to yourself through silver, and can potentially even
enable you to fire the same ability twice in one round.

#### Red

Like with other heroes, your choice of Red really influences how you are playing
the game. That may even be more pronounced here (especially your choice of red
for TII). Not much more to say without getting into branch specifics, so let's
dive in.

**Primary Red: Frost Scepter**

*Target a unit adjacent to you. After the attack: If you target a hero, name a
color. That hero discards a card of that color, if able. End of next turn: That
hero may retrieve that card.*

And with this, the holy trinity of crazy discard abilities is complete. Your
Gold, Primary Blue, and Primary Red make for a very formidable offensive support
with your insane ability to curate your opponent's discard. Much of what was
said about the Primary Blue applies here as well, so I won't go repeating
myself.

While restricted to melee range, this is definitely your more flexible option at
TII, and is what you take if you want to double down on your team fighting
potential.

**Alternate Red: Spirit Bear**

*If you have a card in the discard, target a unit in range. Otherwise, target a
unit adjacent to you.*

*Range 3*

While the card text listed above applies to TIII, I feel like the most important
thing to discuss with this card is in relation to TII, so I'm going to include
that text in full as well.

*If you have a card in the discard, target a minion in range, or a hero adjacent
to you. Otherwise, target a unit adjacent to you.*

*Range 3*

A lot of branches in Guards nudge you in more of a push or kill oriented
direction. However, many still offer some wiggle room in pursuing the other win
condition if necessary. That's not really the case with this card at TII. While
you can technically still hit adjacent heroes, it's low attack value makes it
more of a "discard or be defeated" option than a true attack. The ability to
shoot minions at range 3, especially at the initiative your silver allows, has a
lot of merit, but just be aware that taking this at TII is severly limiting
certain options for you. As such, it might be worth saving this for your last
TII upgrade.

TIII largely gets rid of these problems as the card text loosens it's
restrictions. It becomes a range 3, unrestricted attack that, like your Alt
Blue, is completely in your control to enable.

#### Ultimate: Reign of Winter

*Whenever any enemy hero in play retrieves a card as a result of an action, that
hero discards a card, if able, and you may retrieve your discarded card.*

From reading this ability, we can can see that Gold, Primary Red, and Primary
Blue benefit most from it's effect. However, the ultimate's wording does not
specify enemy heroes retrieving cards as a result of your actions. So in
practice, this card serves to punish all enemy heal options.

The self heal also gives you more opportunity to use the same cards twice in a
row when paired with your Silver, which is nothing to sniff at.

### The Paths

**The Ice Road [-]Trucker [/-]Brawler**

[Image 6943786 on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/6943786)

So I know I said Tali was a support, but this is still Guards...There's really
no room for standing in the back row giving your teammates stat buffs. This
build lets Tali go toe-to-toe with even some of the heavier hitters in the game
while still maintaining that support that makes her great.

**At Tier 2**

We know what we are after so we aren't really wasting time with our Red. Like
most melee choices TII, this gives you a needed defense item that allows you to
better tank hits with your blue, or even your green. It also gets you that
targeted discard ability as quickly as possible. The totem is there more for the
attack item, as we aren't super concerned with initiative here. Then sticking
with the primary blue gives us another targeted discard with another defense
item for padding.

**At Tier 3**

At tier 3, your main goal is just rounding out your stats. Red and Green are
probably interchangeable, I just like the idea of getting that one initiative
earlier if you need it. Switching back to the ice tokens also gives you more
board control. The Red upgrade is mostly self explanatory, but with the two
attack items we plan on having by the end of this, your double discard is not
the only thing your opponent has to worry about. Finally, with an attack that
nice, it would be a shame not to use it twice...so let's pick up that heal.

**The Fantastic Mrs. Fox**

[Image 6943793 on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/6943793)

With our scrapper build out of the way, let's look at an alternative. This is
for a Tali who wants to keep her options open. While it will cost her some
hitting power, she has a kit that can more than make up for it.

**At Tier 2**

The main goal with this tier is to build up to a 14 ini silver (and a 12 ini
blue). Having said that, Blue is a good first level because Tali could always
use a little more defense to not have to block with her Red. Plus, much like the
first build, unlocking tha targeted discard option early really increases your
threat level. Primary Green here is mainly for that initiative item, but we
never say no to some extra tokens. Finally, as discussed in the section about
our Alt Red, we are going with the ranged option here, again mainly for the ini.
We are also getting it last so we can immediately level out of it for our first
TIII.

**At Tier 3**

As we said, we are dropping that TII Red right away with our move to TIII. Next
we are complimenting that with our range item to give us a pretty much
unrestricted range 4 attack. We are also holding onto that targeted discard for
safe measure. While the range gives us the ability to more reliably take 2
minions, our combination of Alt Red and Primary Blue means we can disrupt
enemies from further away. Finally we close out with the totem to synergize with
the pushing power our Alt Red gives us.
