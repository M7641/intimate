## Introduction

[Image 6952347 on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/6952347)

Look at this behemoth. Without reading the cards, you’d probably guess that he’s
a great slow brawler with high attack and defense, right? Well, yes and no. He
does have great attack and defense, but he’s also really fast, he just can’t go
very far. Ursafar the Savage is also the Hulk of the GoACU, he’s either not
angry and not doing much, or he’s mad and wreaking havoc on the enemy. The
bear’s gimmick is that almost all his card can give a **this round** effect that
says you are enraged. All his cards are subpar when you are not enraged, but
amazing when you already are before resolving it. If you like heroes that
require some set up to get going but that are very hard to stop once they get
momentum, Ursafar is for you.

### The Basics

**Gold**

*Before the attack: If you are enraged and not adjacent to an enemy unit, move
up to 1 space. Target a unit adjacent to you. After the attack: This round: You
are enraged*

Remember when I said your cards are subpar when not enraged? This one is an
exception. At 12 initiative and 4 damage, this is the fastest of the high damage
gold attacks cards. You can also use it to get enraged, and if you already are
well you get extra movement and pseudo range, that’s some very sweet golden
honey. If you start a round next to an enemy target, this card is a great way to
open the round, even if general wisdom is to keep your attacks for later.
Getting the early enraged is crucial to Ursafar, and you might even get the card
back later.

**Silver**

*Push enemy minions adjacent to you up to 1 space. If enraged, you*

*may retrieve a resolved attack card. This round: You are enraged.*

You know the common saying: don’t poke the bear? Well I’m pretty sure it came
from this card [**citation needed**]. Notice that it’s a defense and a skill.
You can use it to get enraged. Or you can keep it in hand and your opponents
won’t want to hit you with their low attack cards, because they’ll be afraid of
enabling you or worse let you get an attack card back. Buy a coupe of defense
items and the same thing could apply to their high attack cards too. Don’t poke
the bear indeed.

### The Branches

#### Green

These cards have some very good skills when you are enraged and are either slow
movement or another way to get enraged if you are not.

**Primary Green**

*If not enraged, This round: You are enraged; otherwise, an enemy hero in range,
not*

*adjacent to you, or to another unit, discards a card, or is defeated.*

This the only tool in Ursa’s box that lets you target enemy heroes from far away
(range 3 at tier I, range 4 later). It’s a slow card though so it probably won’t
catch many people off guard, but it’s useful to zone them out, or punish them if
they won’t.

**Alternate Green**

*If not enraged, This round: You are enraged; otherwise, choose one, or both —*

*• Perform an action on your discarded card.*

*• You may retrieve a discarded card.*

This card, especially on tier III is amazing. Self-heal plus use the healed card
twice! Are you kidding me? Of course you desperately want that! Then you look at
the items and realize that to get this card you must give up the movement
upgrade that you desperately want too. That sound you’re hearing in the distance
is Arty laughing maniacally at your agony over this decision.

#### Blue

Both branches are movement primary action, so if you wait to get enraged before
moving, you can move and do something else in addition. That’s neat.

**Primary Blue**

*If enraged, before or after you perform this action, you may swap with a unit
or a token adjacent to you.*

That swapping can sometimes give you a very welcomed boost to your movement.
Smarter than average bears can also use it to set up their teammates’ attacks

**Alternate Blue**

*If enraged, this round: Enemy units cannot move through spaces adjacent to you.
You*

*cannot be placed, pushed, moved, swapped, or forced to move by enemy heroes.
Ignore the effects that limit, reduce, or prevent movement.*

Another good zone out card. It will complicate enemy movement and facilitate
yours. Like many of the bear’s cards, it won’t always do the flashy things on
the text, but your opponents will have to play around it, so that’s a silent
win.

#### Red

**Primary Red**

*Target a unit adjacent to you. Before the attack: If you are enraged and target
a hero,*

*you may remove an enemy minion in radius This round: You are enraged.*

*May repeat once on a different target hero.*

This card can be a sort of double attacks when enraged, and you can repeat it on
tier III. Combine that with your gold and silver, and it’s theoretically
possible for Ursafar to attack heroes 5 times **and** remove 4 minions, in **a
single round**. Good luck pulling this off in the real world, but again the fact
you **can** do it will force the enemy out of their optimal moves.

**Alternate Red**

*Target a unit adjacent to you. After the attack: If you are enraged, and the
target was defeated, gain 3 extra coins; If a hero was defeated, that hero
spends 1 extra Life counter. This round: you are enraged.*

This card is for when the winter is coming. The extra income will help you get a
really fat bear ready for hibernation (just after you finished devouring the
enemy team). No extra attacks on this branch, but better attack and defense
values, so again a tough choice.

#### Ultimate

*You are always enraged. While an active effect of one or more other cards is
also making you enraged, gain +2 Movement.*

You know these two weaknesses that you had before? Well, they’re extinct now. Go
have fun smashing things/people

### The Paths

**The angry hungry bear**

[Image 6952348 on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/6952348)

This is the build for the Ursafar player who just wants to munch on minions and
get their ultimate as fast as possible.

**At Tier 2**

Upgrade red first, this way the extra income starts faster, and you can get
ahead of the other team on levels. Primary blue has a bit more utility in that
build, but if your enemy loves to stack attacks you might switch to alt blue for
the extra defense that you’ll need to maximise your silver.

**At Tier 3**

I love getting my movement upgrade as soon as possible, but again red with extra
income could also be a great first choice here.

**The Prey-dator**

[Image 6952349 on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/6952349)

The other team has decided that it’s bear hunting season? This built will show
them who’s really hunting who. They can’t kill you if you kill them first.
