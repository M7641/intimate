## Introduction

[Image 6925428 on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/6925428)

This is a character guide for Brogan. Brogan is an absolute unit of a brawler,
with heavy damage and strong anti-push. If you like being absolutely unkillable,
one-shotting heroes, all while defending your wave, Brogan is a great place to
start.

### The Basics

**Gold**

*Target a unit adjacent to you. After the attack: Move into the space it
occupied.*

This card is VERY basic. Hit a target, collect coins. Sometimes you can use it
to get one space closer to a minion you want to protect, or closer to a target
you want to hit with your throwing axe, or it opens up a lane for a Mad Dash.
Brogan is naturally very low on movement, using this card in conjunction with
your primary red can shore up those weaknesses in a big way.

**Silver**

*Choose one -*

*- **This Round:** You and friendly units in radius cannot be moved, pushed,
swapped, or forced to move by enemy heroes.*

*- If your discard is empty, retrieve this card.*

The Iconic Brogan card. To understand this card you need to understand the two
main ways heroes die.

- The First way is from a one-shot, the idea is "I hit you so hard you can't do
  anything about it" sometimes its because the defender doesn't have any card
  with that much defense, sometimes its because they played their defense cards
  earlier in the round, they "let their shields down" and got punished for it.

- The Second way is something I like to call a "Card Kill". Somewhere in the
  round someone from your team hits a target with a discard and wounds them, and
  then on turn 4 someone hits them again, often very quickly with a gold card or
  with a red card that has lots of range. Even the tankiest of heroes are very
  vulnerable to this strategy, because everyone has 5 cards.

All this to say, Brogan's silver is great because it shores up his weakness and
makes Card Kills against him unrealistic. He's already great at not dying from
big damage, this card is what makes him a super tank. It also has great synergy
with his alt red and primary green. More cards in hand means its safer to throw
a spear, or allows you to protect more minions. The second effect is a
consolation prize on the turns you play silver and someone hits you, though it
can be very effective in certain matchups.

### The Branches

#### Green

**Primary Green**

***This Round:** If a friendly minion in radius would be defeated, you may
discard a card. If you do, the minion is not defeated.*

Another Iconic Brogan card. Use this card (Even on the first turn of the game!)
to not only stop the enemy team from pushing the wave, but ALSO stop them from
collecting coins. Picking up your silver first also allows you to defend even
more minions, especially on turn 4. If this card is active and you play your
primary red card, you can go slow (7 or 8 INI), defend the minions from the
faster enemy attacks, and then rocket off to get your own minion. Just beware
defending the minions so much that you leave yourself vulnerable to enemy hero
attacks.

**Alternate Green**

*A friendly hero in range may retrieve a resolved or discarded green if either
you, or that hero, is adjacent to an enemy unit.*

The theory crafting green! Imagine all of the cool things you can do with this
card! Give Sabina her red back and let her kill 4 minions! Let Dodger collect
more coins! Bain Hook 2 people! Excitement aside, in practice I've found this
whole category of cards (Cards that heal friendlies but not you) to be pretty
weak, though maybe at higher player counts it gets better. The problem is that
when people see that you can heal others, they simply target you instead.

#### Blue

I'm not going to have a lot to say about Brogans blues, and that's because the
text on both branches are very niche. His defense scaling is 6/7/7, he has a 7
defense at Tier II which is **monstrous.** The best use for this card is often
holding it in your hand and using them like block cards. I suggest choosing your
upgrade based on what item you think would be best, instead of which card.

**Primary Blue**

*Move up to 1 space, then **Choose one -***

*- Remove a token adjacent to you.*

*- Push an enemy unit adjacent to you up to 2 spaces.*

This card can be used to push a minion and open up a lane for charging, or maybe
to send an extra enemy minion towards a team mate that could use the farm.

**Alternate Blue**

*An enemy hero adjacent to you, who has played an attack card this turn discards
a card, or is defeated.*

This card is a third melee attack. Play it when you think weak heroes are going
to hit you, or use it to threaten heroes the turn they try to attack minions.
You can also (At TIII) surprise some wounded targets by attacking with blue and
defeating them before they act.

#### Red

**Primary Red**

*Before the attack: Move 2, 3, or 4 spaces in a straight line to a space
adjacent to an enemy unit, then target that unit.*

This card might look like a killer, but it's actually pretty manageable for the
enemy team. Sometimes you can bluff by setting up this card but choosing a
different one, just the threat of this can really junk up an enemies round.
Sometimes you call the enemies bluff and turn the enemy into mush. Most of the
time your using this to traverse the map, getting an attack and 2+ movement at
the same time is really good. Set up forks where you're threatening to charge in
multiple directions for best effect (Though moving into position is a big tell
that this card is coming). Beware, you HAVE to move 2 spaces in a straight line
before you can attack a target, and enemy heroes will exploit that by going at
higher INI, then running up and body blocking you.

**Alternate Red**

*Before the attack: You may discard a card. +2 range if you have a card in the
discard. Target a unit in range.*

This is the actual killer. Any target that gets wounded will have a hard time
escaping this card, even with as slow as it is. Purchase lots of INI and a Range
item for maximum effect. Even if you're not faster then enemies, you can often
put them in a position of choosing between defeating a minion, or running for
their lives. If they don't run, lots of coins for you. If they do run, well you
get a minion and they don't, win win! **Note:** this is range 1 by itself, so
purchasing an range item will make it range 2 even without a card in the
discard.

#### Ultimate

*You count as 2 minions during minion battle. If you would be removed during
minion combat, you lose the push instead.*

This card is good and it's easy to use. Be in the battlezone at the end of each
round and you will have a VERY hard time losing the game.

### The Paths

**The Beefiest of cakes Path**

[Image 6925403 on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/6925403)

The goal here is to disrupt enemy heroes by moving into straight lines and
scaring them, while traversing the map with red and gold, and saving your
minions with green.

**At Tier 2**

The blue can be exchanged, though the attack item is what makes your red
one-shot and your gold have bite. Green and Red together makes this build work.

**At Tier 3**

The key here is the radius item (which comes with red) giving your green card a
boost, and the TIII green item protecting heavy minions. Once again blues are
interchangeable here.

**The Tanky kill threat path**

[Image 6925401 on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/6925401)

The goal here is to create situations where the enemy team must run from you or
die, allowing you to get ahead on push or kills if they let you. Red is usually
most effective on turn 4.

**At Tier 2**

The greens are interchangeable here. Attack might make your alt red hit
meaningfully harder, but with initiative you can sometimes last hit with your
gold. The Alt blue is useful because it can occasionally give you that extra
discard you need, but honestly either blue is fine (Remember, they're mostly
used for blocking attacks)

**At Tier 3**

This time the we switch blues because the ranged item gives a big boost to your
reds threat. Again, you can choose the primary green instead, but that
initiative is going to give just a little bit more kick to those powerful red
card plays.
