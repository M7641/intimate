## Introduction

[Image 6939955 on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/6939955)

Hello, Eric here with another character spotlight, this time it's Silverarrow
the Pathfinder. Silverarrow is an extremely long-range initiator with strong
movement and area control options. If you want to play keep away with your
opponents while you fill them full of arrows, Silverarrow is the character for
you.

### The Basics

**Gold**

Range 2

*Cannot attack if you are adjacent to an enemy unit. Target a unit in range.
After the attack: You may fast travel, if able.*

Silverarrows gimmick, that her cards don't work if an enemy is adjacent, I like
to call it **"Breathing room"**. The true challenge of Silverarrow is to stay as
close to the action as you can without *ever* allowing enemies to get adjacent.
Enemy heroes that are fast enough (Hanu for example) can walk adjacent to you
before your turn and stop you from attacking with gold or even moving with
silver.

The fast travel gold offers is a very useful tool, and being able to attack AND
fast travel can make you an unstoppable farming machine. There's no shortage of
minions that allow you to defeat them from another zone (3 in the middle zone, 3
on the enemy beach, 2 on your own beach)

**Note:** I'm going to take a moment here to talk a little about Silverarrows
positioning. A lot of people look at her gold card and think "I should be firing
arrows from the enemy backline" but I think that's a trap! Look at this picture
here.

[Image 6934047 on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/6934047)

Each coin represents a space where (Red team) Silverarrow doesn't have breathing
room. The back of the enemy zone is an effective place to farm, and sometimes
that is what's available to you, but it is also fraught with danger. Silverarrow
is at her best, at her safest and most lethal, when she's with her team, close
to the center of the board. She can use terrain and her own minions as
protective barriers, allowing her to target heroes without fear of those heroes
chasing her down. In the picture here she threatens 3 minions with her gold AND
could quickly get an additional +1 defense from her friendly minion if she
needed it.

**Silver**

*If you are not adjacent to an enemy unit, you may move up to 4 spaces in a
straight line to a space not adjacent to an enemy unit*

This is a silver card with FOUR movement on it. It's amazing, there's no silver
card in the game like it. Use it to zip away from an enemy before they can get
to you, use it to traverse large distances in an instance. If you need to get
into the action but are afraid of getting attacked, you can use this card
instead of your blue for movement, which allows you to move without giving up
the defense on your blue.

### The Branches

#### Green

Because of the range on your red and gold cards, your greens are more effective
at setting up your big attacks. If you ever dream of hitting (or killing)
multiple targets with Silverarrows alternate red, mastering your timing with
your green cards is key.

**Primary Green**

*Move an enemy unit adjacent to you up to 3 spaces. If you do, move up to that
number of spaces in a straight line.*

For the enemy team, this mostly reads "Do not end your turn adjacent to
Silverarrow" and is a very strong way to guarantee some breathing room. If an
enemy is foolish enough to try, send them to a spot with lots of negative
defense modifiers and hit them, send them to your teammate so you can fight
together, or send them far far away where they can do no harm. This can also be
used on minions, so look for opportunities to give your teammates farm they
wouldn't get otherwise, or to arrange the minions so your farm comes easier.
Overall a very flexible card that will never let you down.

**Alternate Green**

*If you are not adjacent to an enemy unit, another hero in radius may retrieve a
discarded card.*

If the enemy team doesn't have the legs to keep up with you, or their game plan
is clearly about causing discards, then this is a strong choice. The breathing
room requirement works for you here, if the enemy gets adjacent to you then they
aren't getting adjacent to your friend, so the card does its job either way.
Note that at Tier 2 this card doesn't heal you but at Tier 3 it can heal you
and/or a teammate. You can switch to this card to surprise the enemy team and
win a team fight you wouldn't normally, assuming your teammate can see how
you're supporting them (Or maybe they're just feeding and you save them anyway
:D)

#### Blue

If you want to fire lots of arrows, you either need to be very good at keep
away, or let your blue cards do the defending. Try and make sure your blue
defense can at least protect against the enemies gold cards.

**Primary Blue**

Movement action - 3

*This turn: Enemy heroes in radius adjacent to terrain, or to their friendly
unit, cannot fast travel, or move more than 1 space with a movement action.*

A friend of mine Felix, he has a sticky note on his computer monitor. Before
every game of Guards he plays, he reads the sticky note out loud. Want to know
what it says?

"Check for Silverarrow"

This card is why. Being next to terrain is easy, it happens naturally in this
game all the time. When this card catches two (or even three!) people who
planned to move it can absolutely crush the enemy teams hopes of doing anything
for the round. This card gets kills. This card wins games. On top of the normal
strength of this card, it's also a very effective way to create breathing room,
as long as you have 2 spaces between yourself and the afflicted enemies you're
safe.

**Alternate Blue**

*If you are not adjacent to an enemy unit, End of turn: An enemy hero in radius
discards a card, or is defeated.*

Another excellent blue choice, this card is a reward for being out of reach of a
nearby enemy, or being within three spaces of where they need to go. You can
think of this card as a third attack (It even defeats heroes at Tier 3) and it's
a vital tool when kill victory is your teams win condition.

#### Red

More than most reds, the different branches for Silverarrow have totally
different jobs. Primary red is primarily for killing, and alternate red is
primarily for farming.

**Primary Red**

Range 5

*-2 range for every unit adjacent to the target, including you. Target a unit in
range.*

The way this works is, the more crowded the target, the closer you need to be to
hit it. Don't get confused, this card doesn't care who's adjacent to you, only
who's adjacent to the target. Tier 1 has a weird additional restriction, no
minions unless you're the only unit next to them. Thus, it's a popular choice to
upgrade out of that card first, and it's why stopping Silverarrow from reaching
level 2 is a popular goal for the enemy. At later tiers it's very good at
hitting enemies (Range 5 is absolutely insane) and pretty bad at hitting minions
(for a ranged attack), especially on the beaches where minions are clumped
tightly. Beware, enemy heroes can quickly run over to a minion or a hero and
make your range insufficient.

Choose this card when you're team is getting into lots of fights, and you want
to catch the enemy heroes after they've run away.

**Alternate Red**

Range 3

*Target a unit a maximum range. If you target a hero, may repeat up to two times
on different heroes.*

The dream! Everyone ones to get a triple kill with this card. It has three
primary uses.

A. Initiating on the enemy team. This cards is fast, it can go as high as 13!
Use this card to whack someone on turn 1 or 2 and let your teammate clean up the
kill. Especially effective on targets that can't defend with silver.

B. Farming. You can kill minions from very far away, staying relevant while
maintaining your own breathing room.

C. Last hits. This can happen but don't count on this, you need to be very fast
to hit people on turn 4, especially if they still have their red card. If the
enemy takes any movement at all they can get safe. Consider this a lucky break
you get every once in a while.

**Note:** Beware, picking up a range item with this card. It makes hitting
multiple heroes close to impossible Sometimes, especially with your green, you
can no longer move enough to line up attacks the attacks you want at all. It can
work, especially when you want to affect the opposite side of the zone, but it
can also make the card unwieldy.

#### Ultimate

*Each time before you perform an action you may move 2 spaces in a straight
line.*

This ultimate turns Silverarrow into an absolute menace. She can move before
each attack, and that means she never has to worry about breathing room again,
she creates it each turn before she acts. Enjoy zipping around the map causing
discards, killing heroes and minions with ease. Note that if she wants to move
it has to be exactly 2 spaces, so make sure you always have an escape route.

### The Paths

**The Glassbow**

[Image 6939880 on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/6939880)

The goal here is to consistently take 2 minions OR hit multiple heroes with your
red card. The boots give you the consistency you need to farm, and the attack
makes those turns where you ping players really pack a punch. Finally primary
blue creates the breathing room you need to farm while also disrupting heroes
that need to move a lot.

**At Tier 2**

You could switch the blues if you thought the extra defense would be useful, but
expect to be bullied a little more often if you do. If you know (for sure) that
you're going to win a middle wave, try and get in the enemies spawn zone. You
can farm very effectively there, enemies can't really approach you without
giving up a lot of farm, you're chances of winning a double push will go up
significantly.

**At Tier 3**

Push push push. At this stage of the game you're enemies have probably gotten
quite scary. Your three tools to keep yourself safe are your green (To go after
everyone), your blue (To slow them and keep them away) and your silver (To zip
far far away). Note that 2 of these don't work if you're adjacent to enemies,
keep your breathing room!

**Hawkeye**

[Image 6939926 on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/6939926)

My personal favorite. The whole idea is to start firing arrows and never stop.
The defense and primary green are there to let you get into position safely. The
range and radius is key, giving you a 6 range red, 3 range gold, and even a 4
radius blue attack that kills.

**At Tier 2**

Switch to alt blue if you really want to go hunting early, but the second
defense is important in a lot of matchups, and the slow on primary blue can
still help secure kills if the enemies aren't careful.

**At Tier 3**

The order you level up is challenging here. Grabbing the range item is great
because it makes you better at pushing AND killing. The red gives you range as
scaling and a radius item, making your blue stronger. Your blue gives you a
third kill condition and makes you more survivable with the defense item. This
is all bad because Silverarrow wants them all! But the good news is that it's
flexible, choose whichever upgrade has the most immediate benefit.
