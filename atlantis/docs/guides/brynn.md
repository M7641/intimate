Introduction

Brynn is a trapper, hunter, and mountaineer looking to set up 3 or more things
around a target to get bonus effects on her cards. Lay out objects for opponents
to walk into allowing you to spring the traps! She is a versatile character with
lots of utility and  the ability to be a good farmer, finisher, or support.

[Image 8695182 on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/8695182)

Basics

Before talking about any specific cards Brynn has a theme we have to focus on.

Every card is functional on its own but you have the possibility of getting
bonuses by having an enemy around 3 or more obstacles.

Obstacles are minions terrain, tokens, and heroes including yourself.

I find the easiest way to count this is if they have 3 or less empty spaces
adjacent as it can get confusing counting all the things and terrain hexes.

Gold: Familiar Ground

Choose one —

- Target a unit adjacent to you.

- Target a hero in range who is adjacent to 3 or more obstacles.

( You, other heroes, minions, tokens, and terrain are obstacles. )

.

This is a fast gold which doesn’t hit for a lot but is really good at securing a
kill or hitting someone first.

Brynn uses familiar terrain to spring a hidden trap at 12 ini range 3 if she
hits the bonus.

This is one of her best zoning cards, meaning as long as you are threatening
valuable spaces where heroes would be next to 3 obstacles, you can scare them
away from that space just by staying in range of it usually at turn 3, turn 4,
or you have a lot of attack items.

Silver: Decoy

Choose up to two times, on different targets —

- Move an enemy minion in radius 1 space.

- Move an enemy hero in radius who is adjacent to 3 or more obstacles 1 space.

This card has a few uses:

Brynn can fashion a decoy from her surroundings to have enemy minions
investigate over to a teammate. This will allow the ally to use a card to
possibly disrupt an enemy if they didn’t have enough movement to get 2 minions
otherwise.

This allows you to move the minions in a way that if the enemy walks into
certain spaces in a future turn it will trigger your bonus for cards.

You can use this to immediately put an enemy around 3 or more obstacles.

Move an enemy out of a spot for taking a minion so they have to spend another
card for movement instead of taking a minion.

Big thing is you can use your first option to setup the second option such as
moving a minion so that an enemy hero is around 3 obstacles and then moving that
hero 1 space in the same silver play. The trick is deciding whether it’s worth
more to move the enemy hero or leave the enemy hero setup for 3 or more obstacle
bonus on another card the next turn. In niche cases I’ve used the minions to
block someone's movement or create a mini wall if they play a low movement card.

Branches:

Green

Primary: Bear Trap

Choose one —

- An enemy hero adjacent to you discards a card, if able.

- An enemy hero in radius who is adjacent to 3 or more obstacles discards a
  card, if able.

Brynn uses this bear trap to either throw at someone right next to her or spring
it on somebody who is adjacent to 3 obstacles.

Majority of the time I use this card to dissuade enemies from walking up next to
me.

Sometimes if your allies see it on the table they can walk up next to someone to
make them surrounded by the trap and get a discard at range with someone next to
them to pressure them.

If you are able to use the 3 or more obstacle part of the card you are most
likely going to be able to follow up with a kill or an extra discard with a
follow up gold on the next turn.

Alternate: True Grit

You may retrieve a discarded attack card. If an enemy hero in radius is adjacent
to 3 or more obstacles, move up to 3 spaces.

This card has that anime feel where someone gets punched and they just turn
their head and give you the death stare. Healing a discarded attack is a really
strong effect and you get it regardless of whether or not you hit the bonus of 3
or more obstacles, but if you trigger 3 or more  obstacles you get to reposition
offensively or as an escape is a really sweet bonus. Also the bonus movement is
more than if you were to use the card as a movement.

One thing I see newer players miss is the heal happens regardless if you hit the
bonus or not and then also get to do the extra movement if you get the bonus.

Blue

Primary: Tread Lightly

Swap with either a unit adjacent to you, or with an enemy hero in radius who is
adjacent to 3 or more obstacles.

This card allows you to swap with an ally so they don’t get hit by a red card or
they don’t hit a minion if you want to prioritize wave control.

With the bonus you can gain a lot of ground especially as waves are moving so
you can set your opponent back some time.

There are some niche situations where if your ally can see it you can swap with
an enemy if an ally is sitting next to you and allow them to hit the enemy hero
with their red card.

This card is harder to use when you are on alt red ranged attack because blue
becomes your only good defense card.

Alternate: Mountain Guide

You may move a friendly unit adjacent to you up to 2 spaces. If an enemy hero in
radius is adjacent to 3 or more obstacles, move a different friendly unit in
radius up to 2 spaces.

This allows you to guide a minion or friendly hero through secret terrain that
only you are familiar with a few spaces.

The flashiest use of this is to move someone to allow them to hit an attack they
otherwise couldn’t hit. This requires you and your ally to be in sync with each
other but can feel bad for the other team if they didn’t see this coming.

This is also good for minion denial of the opponents red card.

If you trigger the bonus you can move a second ally minion or hero at range (or
the first if you didn’t have a target with the primary effect because you can
still move someone with the second part)

Red

Primary: High Ground

Target a unit adjacent to you. If you target a hero adjacent to 3 or more
obstacles, +2 attack.

This is secretly: if there is another 2 obstacles because this is a melee attack
you will be one of the obstacles next to the target. You will spring from the
rocks netting you bonus damage if you get the bonus. The bonus damage is very
good especially with such a fast red card. At T3 it gets really bonkers where
you can just pick the card back up again to use for whatever you need like
defending a counter attack or using it to move into a better position.

However this bonus effect doesn’t seem to fire very often but when it does it
feels very rewarding.

Alternate: Split Attack

Target a unit in range. If you target a hero who is adjacent to 3 or more
obstacles, may repeat once on a different unit adjacent to you.

This card allows you to throw your hatchets at range. This is a great choice if
you can’t afford to be next to enemy minions because of the negative defense
modifiers or the fights are happening more around your teammates than you. Range
allows you to be more flexible. You will almost never trigger the effect at Tier
2 but at Tier 3 the usefulness bumps up to being able to hit 2 targets at range
if you can get the bonus.

If you want alt red for the item I like to save it for my last Tier 2 card so I
have the smallest window of the reduced defense.

Playnotes:

Brynn has pretty good statline overall

She doesn’t have to get the bonuses to be effective. Half the fun of playing
Brynn is hunting for those bonus opportunities.

Brynn really benefits from teammates who are familiar with your kit and help you
setup your bonuses. The slower her cards are the more that can change.

If an enemy hero is next to one of your friendly minions they can potentially
take that minion off the board thus turning off previously setup bonuses.

The red card you decide on really dictates your playstyle.

I recommend for your first few games building a brawler style of attack and
defense to get a hang of her and go Primary Red.

Your ability to defend against attacks really takes a nosedive as soon as you
switch to alt red.

You might want to consider taking Primary Blue TII for defense. Also, Alt Green
still has a valuable use in healing Red if you are getting targeted with
discards, not just attacks.

Brynn really relies on her Blue and Red to do the heavy lifting for her defense.
Once those are gone you start to feel pretty scared and have to play very
carefully. Her silver is a very low defense card so I will try to use it at some
point in the round so I’m not left with only a silver on turn 4 in my hand.

Good Teammates

Brynn really likes teammates who can create more tokens on the board or have
abilities to move things to help Brynn setup her bonuses.

Mrak, Tali, Mortimer, Trinkets, Wuk, and Min are some of the top heroes I think
of when looking for a partner for Brynn.
