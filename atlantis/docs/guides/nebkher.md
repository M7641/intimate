## Introduction

[Image 6952786 on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/6952786)

Maybe you’re like me, and prefer the stuff that’s out of this world. Maybe, like
me, you’ve sorted all the heroes and were looking for the most challenging
badass you can play. Or maybe you suffer some natural Guards anxiety and you
just want your Mummy. In any case, you’ve come to the right place, because
NebKher the space-bending, mindcontrolling undead, will show you how to REALLY
play the game. NebKher is a suprisingly tanky, slow INI, shenanigany hero that
specializes in disruption, board control and evil laughter. It’s one of the
heroes who’s cards go all over the place, a bit like Misa. Their kit allows them
to really shine when you need someone in the middle of the board that’s not only
about hitting hard (although this can be achieved) but more about ruining all
the plans of this island’s good citizens, which might include your teammates
once in a while. But do they really deserve a better fate? Mwahahahahahah!

### The Basics

**Gold**

*Choose one —*

*• Perform the primary action of the card in*

*the previous turn slot of an enemy hero in*

*range; apply no item bonuses. If you would*

*place a token, place an illusion token*

*instead. Ignore eff ects that give markers.*

*• Defeat a minion adjacent to you.*

Range 4

This card alone easily justifies the 3 star rating of the Mummy. At range 4, the
capacity to reproduce any enemy’s attack (or any other primary ability) the turn
after those cards were played is creating in and of itself enough headaches to
bend the game in your teams favour. While standing in the middle of the board,
you cover a huge area of denial, often forcing the enemy’s attacks to only
appear during the late round. There are 3 main drawbacks to this card: 1- It is
one of the slowest gold card in the game. 2- You have to stay still while you
take control of someone else. 3- You rely on the enemy’s choice of cards to
actually have 2 attacks during a round. Yet, the simple dread instilled by
having this card in the game is a huge advantage.

**Silver**

*Laugh diabolically. If you do, choose one —*

*• You swap with an illusion token in radius.*

*• You swap with a friendly minion in radius.*

*• You are defeated. Enemies gain no coins.*

*• Next turn: Gain +2 Initiative.*

Radius 4

This card will only work if you laugh diabolically.

For real.

You have to do it. It’s in the rules.

Only then will you have access to one out of four (!) possible actions. The
first 2 are center to NebKher capacity to move. See, this hero is really bad at
walking, since they’re used to teleportation or space-time travel. Walking is
for the weaklings. At 11 INI, this card can really mess the enemy’s capacity to
farm, or even to gain the push, denying minion kill by swapping with them.
Again, the threat of this card alone is lowering the opponents’ mental.

The third point is a super cool denial of coinage if you’re stuck in a pickle in
the middle of the enemy team, which should happen if you play this hero
correctly. Don’t forget to laugh before resolving this one. It’s all about the
mental. You WILL lose life tokens though. So it’s a last resort.

Lastly, if you’re in a safe spot and want to hasten some of your slower cards
for maximum OPness (like your gold!), you can stay put for a turn and have +2
INI during the next one. You are sacrificing your best escape plan so you have
to read the board carefully before using this ability.

Don’t forget that this silver has 4 DEF, the highest defence value for silver
cards in the game.

### The Branches

#### Green

The green cards are both about placing tokens, allowing you to control the
board. Depending on the path you choose, it will enable some form of shenanigany
or the other. Their weird INI values are a double edge sword, in that it will be
faster that most greens, effectively allowing you to disturb other green cards
movements with token placement, but will prevent you from a proper capacity to
chase even the slowest heroes for a late round kill.

**Primary Green**

*Place up to 3 illusion tokens in radius.*

*This round: After an action makes you*

*discard a card, swap with an illusion token,*

*regardless of radius, then remove that token.*

Radius 3

Played early in the round, this card will prevent the enemy team to coordinate
assaults on you by allowing you to teleport out of dodge. Moreover, since you
have complete freedom of where you place the token besides radius, it’s actually
a great way to wall the enemy off of their targets, or just pin them down for
your teammates to finish them. Don’t forget that you do not have a choice, you
HAVE to teleport to a token after being hit. So make sure that there is always
at least 1 token that stands in a spot you’d like to be in for your own plans to
work.

In combination with a high INI build and your silver’s +2 INI, this card can go
before or tied with most red cards, a good way to ruin slower hero’s like Brogan
or Ursafar that rely heavily on their reds to reach the wave after a push.

**Alternate Green**

*Place an illusion token in radius. This*

*round: Illusion tokens are immune to enemy*

*actions. While you are performing actions, the*

*illusion tokens count as friendly melee minions.*

Radius 3

The alt green allows for a single token to be placed, but this token is more
reliable, as it’s immune to enemy actions. It cannot be broken, moved or placed
by heroes like Ursafar, Wuk or Brogan, your main targets for movement denial.
It’s the same INI as the other green so the same weirdness applies, for better
or for worse (even a single token can ruin a slow hero movement), but the main
capacity of this token is to provide DEF modifiers to attacks and to be used in
conjunction to your primary red, which is bonkers. We’ll get to it soon enough.

Going alt green at TII or TIII is also the only way to build high INI for the
late game.

#### Blue

Neb’s blues are high DEF with standard INI numbers. As far as secondary actions
go, they are the most normal cards in the mummy’s deck. Their primary abilities
are far reaching board control skills that are hard to land but powerful in the
right circumstances.

**Primary Blue**

*A hero in radius must swap their unresolved*

*card with another card in their hand, if able.*

*May repeat once on the same Hero..*

Radius 4

You know these moments, where the fate of a game is decided by which card is
played on that one single turn? Neb has some tools to deal with fate, and their
primary blue is where it shines most. At high INI (boosted by silver?), this
card allows the mummy to force a teammate OR an enemy to change the card they
just played with another one, as long as it isn’t resolved yet. Your teammate
will basically get to see what the others play before choosing their own card.
At turn 4, preventing a red card to land on your friendlies can REALLY be a game
changer.

The repeat here is often redundant. Maybe you wanted to see a defence value of a
card in hand before going back to what was played before (this is mostly
valuable on turn 4, where most of the kills will happen). You don’t HAVE to
repeat, and at radius 4 (5 with the upgrade), you cover most of a battlezone.

**Alternate Blue**

*Split the board with a straight line of spaces*

*drawn through your space. This turn: You are*

*immune to actions of units on either side of*

*the line. All other units are immune to actions*

*of units on the opposite side of the line.*

You want to save the life of your miserable allies because they goofed again? Or
maybe you had to take all the hits to allow their miserable plans to go as
intended? There is a simple solution to this. Just break the universe in 2, with
all the enemies on one side, and your friends on the other. And you in the
middle of the nowhere in between.

NebKher alt blue is one of the flashiest abilities in the game, and one of my
personal favorite. It requires good positioning when trying to save allies, but
with infinite range and potentially high INI, it can totally screw up the enemy,
especially ranged heroes like Silverarrow, Dodger, Ignatia, or Swift. Again, if
you combine this with your silver INI boost of +2, it will out INI most of the
attacks in the game.

It can also save your life at TIII, rendering you immune to everyone else,
especially useful if you build the tank mummy build, sitting in the middle of
the fray.

#### Red

NebKher’s reds are both flashy with powerful abilities that are very dependant
on the board state. Their slow INI makes it hard to land reliably, but their
effects are mind bending. Yet again, they instill dread in your enemies.

**Primary Red**

*Choose one, or both, in any order —*

*• Before the attack: Move a friendly minion in*

*range up to 4 spaces to a space adjacent to*

*an enemy hero in range. Target that hero.*

*• Target a minion adjacent to you.*

Range 3

This red card is what made me stick to GoA2 in the first place. The fluff of it
is just so awesome, I wouldn’t mind losing a game just trying to land it.

At TIII, it’s a double attack that allows you to displace your minions, (or that
illusion token on alt green!), walk it towards an enemy in range, and attack
with it. Its attack value of 4 is a lie, since the minion gives -1 DEF to the
hero targeted. Combine this with Sabina’s training drills or Xargatha’s
cultists, plus some ATT upgrades, and any target will quickly find itself
outnumbered and outgunned. Many have died underestimating minions under
NebKher’s influence.

The drawback is of course the minion presence. You can only move friendlies, so
the end of a wave will find you in want of a minion to control. This is where
alt green comes into play.

At TIII, this red’s 8 INI value is bad. You can boost it with silver, but to be
honest, you want to use it on enfeebled targets, or as a surprise high powered
attack.

Again, when in the center of a crowded battlezone, like a pre-throne, you can
defeat an adjacent enemy minion, and then move one of yours, effectively
depriving a target of 2 DEF bonus, especially against melee enemies who stick
too close to your taste.

**Alternate Red**

*Target a unit in range. After the attack:*

*If you target a hero, you may swap*

*any two of their resolved, unresolved,*

*or discarded cards.*

Range 2

This is a very slow ranged 2 attack that is mostly used for either farming
reliably, or to empower your gold card. Don’t forget that you can get a range
upgrade that will affect both this red and your gold, and the ability to
reorganize the enemy’s resolved, unresolved!, or discarded!! cards after they
tanked a 5+ ATT makes for a huge area of denial around you on turn 3 or 4.

#### Ultimate

*If you would be defeated as part of your own*

*action, first defeat all enemy minions in radius*

*and enemy heroes in radius discard a card, or*

*are defeated. Spend no life counters.*

This is where the diabolical laughter will come easily. The only real area
damage of the game, this card allows kills on ALL minions AND damage or defeat
ALL heroes in radius 2, boosted to 3 with an upgrade. You have to suicide using
your silver in order to do so, but the cost in life counters is now gone for
good. Reaching the utlimate is hard on NebKher since your attacks are generally
conditional, but if you do, death becomes your ally, and winning a final push or
depleting the enemy’s final life counters in a turn 4 apocalypse is easier than
ever.

### The Paths

**I will eat your soul**

[Image 6952778 on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/6952778)

The goal of this build is to position agressively for maximum effect on your
gold card. It's a build that will work especially well vs long range pingers,
but is also effective against high damage melee fighters. The high DEF high INI
will allow you to setup reds and golds to good effect, while protecting you
against one-shots, or late round kills with your TIII alt blue. The extra boot
and range keep your zone of influence huge, but at the cost of high attack
values, since attack upgrades are void with gold anyways.

**At Tier 2**

Rotating to alt red first will greatly increase your farm capacity. Or, you can
upgrade green first if you need the extra INI, but in any case get blue last,
cause it's not gonna save your own life at TII.

**At Tier 3**

This is where you start to shine. First, get that extra range upgrade, unless
you really need survivability, in that case go for alt blue right away. If you
do that, you'll want to get INI quickly after by taking green. Taking red last
is ok, since you wanna use it mainly to threaten with gold, and the TII ability
is still very strong at range 3.

**The colours out of space**

[Image 6952779 on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/6952779)

This is the build I'd go for maximum disruption, which happens to be maximum
damage as well. Your survivability is linked to your greens and your silver, and
you become dependant on friendly minions late game. So your team needs to know
how to complement you more than usual. The payoff is an 8(\*)-ATT range-4 red at
up to 11 INI. Your gold is used for early round pings or copying disruptive
blues and greens, and you red for finisher moves. (\*Minion DEF modifier
included)

**At Tier 2**

As with the other build, you might want to prioritize taking alt red right away
for secure farming. It's also tied with an INI item, which you really want to
secure good silver escapes early game. Then it's up to you. The higher damage
value you get by upgrading to TII primary green is not gonna shine until your
rotate back at TIII red, so there is no rush, except that placing two illusion
tokens is twice as good as placing just one. Go blue second or last, depending
on the push situation. This card will give some serious headache to the enemy if
they try to secure the push/kill at the end of round 2 or 3.

**At Tier 3**

Now all options are really dependant on the board state here, but one thing is
for sure, try to hide your rotation to primary red as much as possible. You get
+1 range on the master of puppets, which the enemy will probably miscalculate if
you've done your homework breaking their mental early game. Getting the radius
upgrade also makes most of your cards better. Upgrade blue early if you want
that extra humph with the ATT upgrade, but the INI stays the same and the
ability doesn't scale really. I suggest going for alt green if you feel
pressured by either higher INI enemies or dead minions everywhere you need them
to be alive. You will lose the range, but it's better to be range 3 and useful
than range 4 and outpaced.

[Image 6952845 on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/image/6952845)

NebKher is a very complex hero that especially shines in 3v3s or 5v5s. They are
a headache to deal with for the enemy and very tanky for a mage-like hero. Use
this to your advantage and you won't be able to stop LAUGHING.
