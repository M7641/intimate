## Introduction

As requested, the next spotlight from me is Tigerclaw the Cutpurse. If you like
these and want to see more message below with who you'd like to see next.

Tigerclaw is an extremely powerful initiator with access to farm, escape, and
speed. If you’ve ever loved Talon, Loki, or Riki from you favorite MOBA, or just
generally love infuriating your enemies, Tigerclaw is on the short list of
characters you need to try. Tigerclaw is also probably THE best tutorial hero in
the game IMO. Give this hero to new players to give the best chance of them
coming back to play again.

**Note:** I have strong opinions on the game, and for Tigerclaw in particular,
my opinions are infamous on the Wolffdesigna discord. Read all of this at your
own risk.

### The Basics

**Gold**

At 13 INI, this is the fastest card in the game. Use this card to farm, or dash
through an enemy hero to start or (more likely) finish a fight before they can
do anything to stop you. Beware, the dash on this card is predictable and clever
enemies will threaten your landing point.

**Silver**

This card might not look like much, but it’s busted. The slow initiative means
you get to see what everyone else does, then make the best decision with that
information. The "Next Turn" effect means you can’t be attacked, swapped,
pushed, moved, or generally interacted with, Unrestricted immunity is VERY, VERY
good. Use this all the time, every round if you can, but especially on the turn
you want to threaten the area around you, or when you think someone might start
chasing you.

### The Branches

#### Green

These cards are where all of your non-combat punch is since your blues ONLY
protect you. At 2/1/1 initiative you’re green cards are the slowest in the game
(This is a good thing), and 3 movement is the highest value green cards can
have. Tigerclaw really has it all.

**Primary Green**

You upgrade INTO this first and OUT of it last. This card has basically 4
actions in one.

1. Move 2 spaces

2. Remove 2 of the enemies coins.

3. Gain 2 coins

4. Move 2 additional spaces

It's wayyyyyy too much value for a green card. You can get enemy heroes
seriously behind by removing the coins, and get seriously ahead at the same
time. This card also works well with silver, because you can move through units
before and after stealing their coin.

**Alternate Green**

This is your last upgrade. At that point most heroes will have built so many
items (Assuming you let them) that reversing those items will at worst make them
run for their life, at best it will make the character dysfunctional.

#### Blue

Your blue INI is 10/11/11, you're gold and blue both can escape fast threats.

Both of Tigerclaws blue cards are block cards. Picking the right one, and then
holding that card in your hand until the right moment is the key to getting the
most out of this hero.

**Primary Blue**

This is the correct blue card something like 70% of the time. Heroes can’t
really keep pace with Tigerclaws premium movement and initiative. The only way
they can consistently threaten him is with ranged attacks. Occasionally you will
have a melee fighter that wants to try and fight you, but a lot of the time in
those situations your blue (11 at Tier 2) just goes first, which makes this blue
the best at defending from both types of attacks.

**Alternate Blue**

There are three reasons to take this blue.

A. The enemy is all melee attacks (And can’t/won’t switch into ranged)

B. You think the only real threat to you is a pseudo-ranged attack, like
Brogan’s charge, or Whispers lunge.

C. You think you can trick a target until killing themselves with this discard.

The more experienced the group, the less likely C will work, and the first time
you play this card after levelling up the surprise is spoiled.

#### Red

At 9/9/10 these attacks happen fast. For best effect, play these cards after the
slow cards that set them up (Silver and Green)

Primary Red

At Tier 2 this card is the correct choice. The reason it is correct is that it
allows you to attack the turn after you play silver. Play this on the turn
you’re immune allows you to attack heroes or minions without fear of
retaliation, and the movement you get before the attack means you’re more likely
to connect.

At Tier 3 there’s nothing flashy here, just good value. You can do in 1 turn
(Attack and move 2 spaces) what most heroes have to do in 2 turns.

**Alternate Red**

Choose this card at level 2 if you’d like to play Tigerclaw on hard mode, maybe
if your friend is playing for the first time or you want to show off. Choose
this card at level 3 if you already have a ranged item from blue (Trivia: This
is the only card in the game that is NOT ranged at tier 2 but IS ranged at Tier
3). It’s a lot of damage, and can catch people off guard if you switch from
primary red to this card and they don’t notice. There are some support heroes
that can guarantee this attack (Bain hook is just one example) but that takes a
lot of coordination and team work, eww.

#### Ultimate

The turn after you play silver, play gold. You can move 2 spaces then dash
through a target, then move 2 spaces and dash through a totally different
target. This lets you kill the 5th wave heavy minion insanely quickly, or mix it
up and play alt red after silver to surprise someone by moving 2 spaces and
backstabbing them. As far as ultimates go, it’s not the strongest (all heroes
with additional farm cards have the weaker ultimates) but it does the work.
